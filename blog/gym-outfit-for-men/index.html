Gym Outfits for Men: Clean, Simple, No Overthinking

This article contains affiliate links (advertising). The products are a curated selection, not test reports.

Most good gym outfits for men are built from a few basics in black, grey and white. That way everything goes with everything.

The basics

  • 2–3 fitted or regular training shirts
  • Shorts or joggers
  • A hoodie or zip jacket for the way there
  • Simple sneakers and sports socks

Colors

Stick to black, grey and white. An all black outfit always looks clean, and grey sweatpants with a white tee are the streetwear classic.

Fit

Compression shirts fit tight, regular training shirts sit looser. Baggy sweatpants are great for everyday wear after training.
