Most good gym outfits for men are built from a few basics in black, grey and white. That way everything goes with everything.

The basics

  • 2–3 fitted or regular training shirts
  • Shorts or joggers
  • A hoodie or zip jacket for the way there
  • Simple sneakers and sports socks
