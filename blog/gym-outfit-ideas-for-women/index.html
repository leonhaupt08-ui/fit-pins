Gym Outfit Ideas for Women: 4 Looks That Always Work

This article contains affiliate links (advertising). The products are a curated selection, not test reports.

A good gym outfit doesn't have to be complicated. These four looks work for training and on the way there.

1. The matching set

Top and leggings in the same color instantly look put together. Dark grey, black and brown are easy to combine.

2. All black

Black leggings, black top, white socks and white sneakers – the classic that never goes wrong.

3. Shorts instead of leggings

For cardio and warm days: high waisted shorts with a sports bra or crop top.

4. Gym to street

Throw a zip jacket or an oversized hoodie over your set and you're ready for the way home.
