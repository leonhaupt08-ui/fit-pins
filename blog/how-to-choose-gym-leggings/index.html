Gym Leggings: What to Look for Before You Buy

This article contains affiliate links (advertising). The products are a curated selection, not test reports.

Leggings are the part of your gym outfit that has to handle the most: squats, lunges, cardio. Before you buy, check these four things.

1. Squat proof

Stretch the fabric in front of a light or do a deep squat in front of a mirror. Thicker, matte fabrics are usually more opaque than thin, shiny ones.

2. High waist

A high waistband stays in place better when you move and is easy to pair with crop tops and sports bras.

3. Seams and fit

Seamless leggings have hardly any seams and chafe less. Scrunch styles shape the look but are a matter of taste.

4. Pockets

Side pockets for your phone are more useful for running and the gym than you might think.
