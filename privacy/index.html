Privacy Policy – Fit Essentials Pin Manager

Last updated: November 2026

What this app does

Fit Essentials Pin Manager is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pinterest account "Lildesports" to create Pins and read analytics for that one account. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

With the account owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Pinterest boards, Pins and account analytics through the official Pinterest API. It does not access data of other Pinterest users.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's Pins and to review the performance of the owner's own content. Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No personal data of visitors is collected on this website, and no cookies or tracking are used here.

Affiliate links

Pins published by this account may contain affiliate links (Werbung). Clicking such a link takes you to the retailer's website, where the retailer's own privacy policy applies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Pinterest account settings under "Apps".

Contact

Questions: via Instagram @fit.finds.de.
